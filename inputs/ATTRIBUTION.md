# Evaluation catalog

This offline catalog and its artwork are carried forward from the approved Folio prototype. Original catalog records came from the Pokémon TCG API community data repository: https://github.com/PokemonTCG/pokemon-tcg-data . Original card images were served at https://images.pokemontcg.io/ . Catalog records are identified by their original set/card IDs.

Artwork copyright belongs to Pokémon, Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK and the respective rights holders. No ownership or production image license is claimed. These are evaluation assets for the private prototype; public distribution requires the project's content-rights assessment.

The dataset includes Expedition Base Set (165 designs) and 12 selected modern designs. Full set totals are metadata, not an assertion that all cards are present. No historical market prices or vendor pricing dataset is bundled. Demonstration valuations are fabricated inside FolioStore and explicitly labeled as examples in the interface.
